Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Pickup orders are ready in 1–2 hours. Campus delivery takes 24–48 hours.

Can I return a product?

Yes — unused items can be returned within 7 days of purchase. Contact support to start a return.

How do I track my order?

Visit the Cart page and click on any order to see its current status.

Do you deliver outside LASU campus?

Delivery is currently limited to LASU Ojo campus and surrounding areas.
